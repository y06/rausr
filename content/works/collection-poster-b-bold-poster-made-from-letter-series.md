### Project Overview

BOLD is a typographic poster from a single-letter poster series. Each artwork in the series starts with one letter and pushes it until the character becomes the whole composition, not just a piece of written language.

### Poster System
#### One letter as the complete image

The letter B is repeated, enlarged, cropped, and layered until it turns into a graphic structure. Instead of adding illustration around the typography, the poster lets the letter build the full visual field: rhythm, weight, depth, and movement all come from the same basic character.

{{< quote >}}
The idea is to make one letter carry the whole poster, so typography becomes image, structure, and topic at the same time.
{{< /quote >}}

### Color and Variants
#### Bold form, shifting atmosphere

The series tests the same letter-based system across green, pink, and yellow color versions. The large B shape stays dominant, while gradients and layered transparency change the mood of each composition. Some variants feel more electric and sharp, others more warm, decorative, or interior-friendly.

### Collection Use
#### A typographic study with a simple rule

This collection works as a compact funny experiment in constraint-based poster design. By limiting the visual language to one letter, the design becomes more focused: every curve, counter-shape, overlap, and edge has to do more work. The result is a poster series about typography as a subject, not only as a tool for writing.
