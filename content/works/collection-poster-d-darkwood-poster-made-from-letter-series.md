### Project Overview

DARKWOOD is another piece from the single-letter poster series. This time the system is built around the letter D (as d version), using its heavy curve and vertical stem as the base for a dark, layered composition made for a quiet interior mood.

### Poster System
#### One letter turned into atmosphere

The letter D is repeated, enlarged, cropped, and pushed into the edges of the poster. Instead of treating typography as a caption, the letter becomes the main image. Its repeated forms create depth, rhythm, and a slightly hidden structure that only slowly reveals itself.

{{< quote >}}
The poster keeps one simple rule again: use a single letter until it becomes the full visual world.
{{< /quote >}}

### Mood and Color
#### Dark tones with small cuts of light

The palette works with black, deep blue, muted violet, and green light (or in second version with more natural yellow light). The result feels more interior, nocturnal, and wood-toned than the brighter parts of the series. Small bright openings inside the letterforms help the composition breathe while keeping the overall character calm and dense.

### Collection Use
#### A typographic object for darker spaces

This case study shows how the same letter-series idea can shift into a different emotional register. DARKWOOD is less loud and more atmospheric, designed to sit naturally in darker rooms, warm wood settings, and interiors where the poster should feel bold without becoming noisy.
