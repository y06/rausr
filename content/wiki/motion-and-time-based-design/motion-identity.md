## What it is

Motion identity translates brand character into behaviour. Useful systems describe principles—such as direct, elastic, precise, layered, or calm—and show how those principles affect recurring components.

## Components of the system

### Movement principles

Principles connect strategy to execution. They guide direction, acceleration, rhythm, transformation, and the relationship between elements without prescribing one identical animation everywhere.

### Timing and easing

Duration changes meaning. Fast movement can feel responsive or urgent; slow movement can feel calm or obstructive. Easing communicates weight and intention, while linear movement is better suited to continuous or measured processes.

### Brand assets in time

Logos, type, colour fields, crops, patterns, characters, and sound can enter, transform, or respond consistently. The system should define restrained everyday behaviour as well as expressive campaign moments.

## Practical checks

- Test silent viewing and sound-on viewing.
- Define reduced-motion alternatives for non-essential movement.
- Check that functional transitions clarify state rather than delaying it.
- Create templates for common ratios, durations, and production teams.
- Judge recognition across a sequence, not only on the final frame.

## Common mistake

Calling a single logo reveal a motion identity. A reveal is one asset. A motion identity is a set of reusable decisions that can produce many coherent assets across products, campaigns, presentations, and film.
